I co-founded [Asket Studio](https://asketstudio.in) and work on a project from the first wireframe to the day it ranks: UI/UX design in Figma, the build, and the SEO after launch. Most of our clients are manufacturers (tiles, solar, agriculture) who need a product catalogue on the web that loads fast and is easy to browse.

| Design | Web | Apps | SEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| UI/UX, design systems and app interfaces in Figma | Catalogue and brand sites in PHP and JavaScript, motion with GSAP | Android apps in Kotlin and Material 3 | Technical audits, site structure, schema, Search Console |
